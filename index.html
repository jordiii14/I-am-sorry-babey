First, from my heart

A letter for you, Wifey 🤍

My Wifeyyyyy,

I know I’ve hurt you, especially by not keeping my promise, and I understand why my words don’t feel as believable as they used to. I’m not asking you to forget what happened or to suddenly trust me again. You deserved consistency from me, and I know I haven’t always given you that.

With the wedding, family things, studies and everything else going on, I let our time slip instead of making you feel considered. Those things explain where my time went, but they don’t erase how it felt for you. You matter to me even on the busiest days, and I want to get better at showing that instead of just saying it.

I still love you, and I still hope that one day, if we both choose it and God leads us there, I’ll get to call you my wife. I want a relationship where we can pray together, laugh together, grow together, and feel safe being honest with each other. I know I have to build that through everyday actions, not one letter.

I miss our little moments and the excitement we had when everything was new. I don’t expect you to smile or be affectionate before you’re ready. I just want to make space for good moments again, at your pace, without pretending the hurt never happened.

So I wanted to ask you something gently…

A question, with no pressure

Would you let me court you again? 🥹

Not asking you to erase our history or give me instant trust. I’m asking if you’d be open to letting me show you, little by little, what loving you with more intention can look like—with God at the centre, and honesty and patience between us.

You can say yes, no, or that you need more time. I’ll respect your answer.

Only if you feel comfortable

Can I take you on a little bingo date? 🎟️

I remember you’ve wanted to play bingo with me for a while, and I still want to watch a movie with you too. Could we pick a time next week to play bingo, choose a movie, and just enjoy each other’s company? No pressure to make everything perfect.

And a video call? 📱

When your parents aren’t around and you feel comfortable, I’d really love to video call you and talk properly—not just text. You can tell me when you have privacy and feel up to it. I don’t want you to feel pressured to call before you’re ready.

One last thing
